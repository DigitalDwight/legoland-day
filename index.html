The one rule for today

Most rides a 90 to 95 cm child can do have a 0.9 m minimum, and an adult must ride with them. Staff measure at the entrance to each ride. If the first measure comes out under 0.9 m, drop the rides marked "0.9 m min" and keep the ones marked "no min". Everything marked 1.0 m or taller is a no today.

Tips

  • Mythica, at the east end, has nothing they can ride. Skipping it saves about 40 minutes of walking.
  • The park sits on a hill. Use the Hill Train to go down and back up, and leave Miniland and the shops until the end, near the exit.
  • Splash Safari and Drench Towers are water play areas. In late September they may be closed or too cold, so bring spare clothes if you plan to try.
  • Haunted House Monster Party is dark and loud. It's fine for some 4 year olds and too much for others.
  • Lunch from 12:30 to 13:00 is the busiest time at every restaurant. Eating at 13:15 or later cuts the wait.
